2. What Splitty is

Splitty is a bill-splitting calculator with a shared web page. You type in the items on a bill (or, with Pro, snap a photo of the receipt and let AI read it), add tax and tip, and get a link. Friends open it, tap the items they had, and everyone's share updates live on every phone. If you add a Venmo, Cash App or PayPal handle, people who owe you get a Pay button with their amount filled in and can mark themselves paid.

Shared items split by how many of them each person claimed. Tax and tip spread in proportion to what each person owes, and the part belonging to unclaimed items stays unassigned until someone claims them. Everything rounds to the cent. Bills are in US dollars only, and a bill holds up to 100 items and 30 people.

Who owes what, and whether they pay, is between you and your friends. Splitty just does the math.

4. Bill links: anyone with the link is in

A bill lives at a link like splitty.cc/b/… ending in a long random code. The link is the key: there is no password and no guest list.

  • Anyone with the link can open the bill and see everything on it. That means: the name, items and prices; who has joined and what they claimed; who's marked paid and when; which person is the creator; whether it's locked; when it was created and last changed; and the creator's payment handles. All of it is also available as raw data, not just on the page.
  • Anyone with the link can join under any name, without an account, and claim items for that name. Names aren't verified. Anyone can also just watch.
  • Links are long and random so they can't be guessed, but forwarding the link forwards all of that, and we can't take a link back once it's out. Share it with the people at the table, not in public.

The person who created the bill (the creator) can edit the bill name, items, tax and tip; add or clear payment handles; claim items and mark paid for anyone on the bill; and lock or unlock it. On a locked bill nobody new can join and nobody but the creator can change claims; people can still mark themselves paid, and everyone with the link can still see the bill. The bill's live connection also lets the creator rename or remove anyone on the bill (removing someone clears their claims and paid mark), and lets you rename or remove yourself; there are no buttons for this yet.

Creator powers are tied to a secret token your browser keeps. The backup edit link under creator tools contains that token: anyone you give it to gets full creator control, so treat it like a password. If your browser forgets the token, that link is the only way back in; we can't restore it. The same goes for people who join: your place on a bill is tied to a token your browser keeps. If it's lost, join again under a new name or ask the creator to claim or mark paid for you.
